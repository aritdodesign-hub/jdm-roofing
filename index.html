Who We Roof

Churches, Schools & Businesses

With an unlimited license we take on roofs of every size, from a small church to schools, government buildings, industrial plants and shopping centers.

Churches · Metal & Shingle

Steeples, Sanctuaries & Fellowship Halls

Church roofs mix steep pitches, steeples and long flat wings. We roof all of it, from shingles and metal panels to the custom flashing our own shop bends to fit every corner.

  • New roofs and re-roofs
  • Custom-fabricated metal flashing
  • Gutters and downspouts

Schools · Government

Public Buildings, Built to Spec

Schools and government buildings need a roofer that is licensed, insured and bonded, and that finishes on schedule. Built-up and membrane systems keep big low-slope roofs dry for the long haul.

  • Built-up, TPO, EPDM and modified bitumen
  • Licensed, insured and bonded
  • Roof surveys and maintenance programs

Commercial · Industrial

Shopping Centers & Industrial Plants

When we finish a commercial or industrial roof, the building looks brand new. We re-roof and retro-fit occupied buildings around rooftop units, so the business inside keeps running.

  • Re-roofs and retro-fits
  • Metal roofs and metal wall panels
  • Warranties on the roofing products
